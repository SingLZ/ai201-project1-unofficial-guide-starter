Title: Apartment Reviews Advice
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/SanJose/comments/vxoux5/apartment_reviews_advice/
Type: Reddit thread

Original Post:
The poster is moving to San Jose and wants housing advice near downtown. They are looking west of 87 toward Santa Clara and started applying to Mosaic. They noticed bad Google and Yelp reviews for Mosaic and nearby apartments like 808 and Elements. Complaints include management problems and car break-ins in garages. The poster has a rough budget of $3,500 for a two-bedroom and wants to know how to judge whether newer luxury apartments are worth it.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said they had a very negative experience with Mosaic, Standard, and Elements, all managed by Greystar. They described the leasing office as undertrained and unresponsive, with poor communication to residents.

[COMMENT]
The same commenter said motorcycles were stolen multiple times from a garage with cameras. They said the leasing office claimed they would review footage but never followed up beyond requesting a police report number.

[COMMENT]
The same commenter said move-out cleaning fees were very high and that the deposit was consumed with extra charges. They also said they had lived at an Avalon apartment and did not have problems there.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said they rented with Greystar once and also did some business with them, and considered the company very poor.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said many new buildings are corporate housing with weak management. They said office calls may get routed to out-of-area call centers that only promise a response within 48–72 hours, including for maintenance.

[COMMENT]
A commenter advised looking for a smaller updated complex because response times may be better. They suggested that 4–16 units can be a useful range, though it depends on the individual property.

[COMMENT]
Another commenter agreed that a smaller, less corporate apartment complex can be a better bet. They suggested expanding the search north of downtown toward Japantown, which they described as nice, safe, mixed-income, and walkable.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said renting always involves some complaints, but car break-ins, bike theft, and catalytic converter theft are risks across San Jose regardless of whether someone lives in an apartment or house.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said they lived east of 87 by San Pedro Square. They knew of only minor incidents such as theft of community TVs and trespassing. They said management responded quickly and the building hosted monthly tenant events.

[COMMENT]
A commenter advised reading complaints carefully because people often leave negative reviews after a single bad incident. The implication is that renters should look for repeated patterns, not isolated complaints.

[COMMENT]
Overall thread pattern: the main concerns are garage security, management responsiveness, corporate ownership, move-out fees, and how much weight to give negative online reviews.
