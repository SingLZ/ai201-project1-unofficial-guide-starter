Cleaned source notes:
San José State University maintains an Off Campus Housing Resources page through University Housing Services. The page is meant as an informational resource for students who are considering housing outside university-managed residence halls.

The page includes a disclaimer that students should evaluate off-campus housing carefully before making legal commitments. SJSU states that off-campus housing information is informational and that tenant-landlord agreements are the responsibility of the tenant and landlord.

For this guide, this source is useful because it provides official university context: students should not treat informal housing resources as legal advice or guaranteed endorsements. It supports answers about caution, lease review, and the limits of university involvement in off-campus housing.

Relevant housing factors for students include lease terms, landlord responsibilities, tenant responsibilities, safety, commute to campus or work, parking, and whether the source is official or informal.

This source should be treated differently from Reddit comments. It does not provide renter opinions about specific complexes, but it provides a formal warning to assess off-campus housing carefully and understand legal responsibility before signing.
