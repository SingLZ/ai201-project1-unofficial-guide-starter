Title: 1b1b Apartment Recommendations near south San Jose
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/SanJose/comments/1j0k6gn/1b1b_apartment_recommendations_near_south_san_jose/
Type: Reddit thread

Original Post:
Hello! I am a new graduate moving to San Jose area. I am looking for 1b1b apartments near south San Jose. Budget is around 3.5k. I just really want somewhere that is safe. It would be even better if it is convenient for me to get groceries :3 Any recommendations?

[COMMENT]
You might want to post up what your definition of South SJ is. Some 3 bedroom homes in SSJ rent for $3k for context. SSJ is very suburban and boring for a young grad. A fair bit of traffic to get to where most offices are too.

[COMMENT]
The poster clarified that the office is close to the Santa Teresa area and they wanted a not-too-long drive. A reply said Santa Teresa is pretty but not much is happening there, although it is easier to drive somewhere fun than deal with rush-hour traffic.

[COMMENT]
A commenter living in Santa Teresa said there is not much there and suggested living in Campbell while working in Santa Teresa. They said the commute would mostly be counter commute, and Campbell has more fun things to do.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said reverse commute is real: southbound roads are not busy in the morning and northbound is light in the evening. They said Santa Teresa can be reached from many parts of the city in about 15 minutes.

[COMMENT]
A commenter described the Santa Teresa area as sleepy suburbia. They said with a $3.5k budget, the renter could consider Campbell, Japantown, Willow Glen, or downtown San Jose.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said they were leaving a 1b1b loft at Duo Apartments off Cottle and Santa Teresa. They said the rent was being raised to about $3,500.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended SoFi Waterford Park. The original poster asked about traffic around that area.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Villa Veneto because units come with a garage. They also recommended Santa Palmia, Palma Sorrento, and Ascent Apartments. They said these complexes are near Cottle and close to Safeway.

[COMMENT]
A commenter suggested apartments near Cottle Road, including Ascent Apartments, Monterey Grove, and Urban One.

[COMMENT]
A commenter suggested Santa Clara because utilities can be cheaper than San Jose. They recommended looking at apartments near the southern end of Santa Clara, bordering West San Jose and Cupertino.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Fruitdale Station Apartments because it is across from light rail and has a grocery store next door.

[COMMENT]
A commenter warned that the area around Branham and Meridian is known for cars being stolen and broken into. They said they used to live there and saw problems frequently.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended downtown Morgan Hill if the renter wants to be close to South San Jose but maybe outside San Jose itself.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Avalon Willow Glen, saying they lived there for two years, had a fantastic experience, and it was close to Santa Teresa.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said a few coworkers live in The Woods Apartments. They described it as a gated neighborhood around $2,500 and estimated the commute to Santa Teresa at 10–15 minutes. Another commenter strongly disagreed and said they recently moved out and did not like the complex.
