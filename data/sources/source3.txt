Title: Recommended neighborhoods to rent an apartment?
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/SanJose/comments/1s39tcf/recommended_neighborhoods_to_rent_an_apartment/
Type: Reddit thread

Original Post:
The poster received a San Jose job offer and would move from the East Coast. They would work near Santa Teresa and want to keep the commute under an hour. They prefer an urban environment but care most about cost and neighborhood quality. They like walkable areas similar to New York City, will have a car, will have two cats, and want to stay under $3,000 if possible. They are considering Japantown, Willow Glen, and Almaden Valley.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Campbell. They said the budget might be tight, but downtown Campbell or the Pruneyard is walkable, has bars and restaurants, a Sunday farmers market, and summer festivals. They said it is within a one-hour commute of Santa Teresa.

[COMMENT]
Another commenter also recommended Campbell. They said it is about 20 minutes to the Santa Teresa area, has a cute downtown, several grocery stores nearby, and access to hiking areas.

[COMMENT]
When asked about downsides to Campbell, commenters said price is the main downside. Walkable and nice locations are in high demand and can cost a premium.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said Avalon Campbell was their first apartment after moving from the East Coast. They liked the area near downtown Campbell, the Pruneyard, restaurants, coffee shops, bars, stores, bookstores, the library, and the Sunday farmers market. They said one downside may be that it is not a late-night area.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said Japantown is great and very walkable. They said downtown San Jose is the most walkable area with the most restaurants, nightlife, and cultural events, but people may encounter homelessness there.

[COMMENT]
The same commenter said Campbell is also strong for bars and restaurants, but to be walkable one probably needs to live near downtown Campbell. They also said a downtown or Japantown commute might be a reverse commute depending on work location.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said walkability is limited in San Jose except for a few downtown blocks. They said surrounding cities have main streets, but residents often need to drive to them.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said South Bay has strong access to hiking, with many trails within around 30 minutes regardless of where someone lives.

[COMMENT]
A commenter warned that San Jose is not like New York City and will not offer the same density or amenities. They suggested Santa Clara near Santana Row as a possible option.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Campbell near the Pruneyard, downtown Campbell, or downtown Los Gatos. They said both are pricey but people pay for walkability and amenities.

[COMMENT]
A commenter compared Campbell and Willow Glen. They said both are walkable, but Campbell has more variety, nightlife, and Los Gatos Creek Trail access. Willow Glen is more established, closes earlier, and feels closer to downtown San Jose.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said Campbell is better for people who still want bars after 9:30 p.m. and want to walk. They missed Campbell’s farmers market, coffee shops, later hours, younger people, the Pruneyard, and Los Gatos Creek Trail after moving.

[COMMENT]
A commenter suggested apartment complexes at Cottle and Highway 85 in Santa Teresa. They said the commute would be short, with grocery stores and fast casual restaurants nearby. The area is suburban and not exciting, but convenient and freeway-accessible.

[COMMENT]
Another commenter said the Cottle and 85 area is close to work and convenient but boring. They said they would not pick it unless they could not afford a more fun area.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said the simplest strategy is to rent close to work, ideally walkable to work. Otherwise, the renter may have to choose between a good neighborhood and a somewhat walkable area that is farther from work.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended St. Leo’s / Diridon. They highlighted The Alameda, restaurants, shops, Whole Foods, bookstores, artist studios, access to 87 and 280, a short commute to Santa Teresa, Caltrain to San Francisco, and VTA to Campbell.

[COMMENT]
A commenter listed Santana Row, The Alameda around Diridon, Campbell, and Willow Glen as options, but warned that none of them will feel like New York City.

[COMMENT]
A commenter reminded the poster to check pet fees for two cats when comparing rent.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Campbell, Los Gatos, or Willow Glen for cute downtown areas, restaurants, bars, parks, and hiking access. They said the Santa Teresa commute would be reasonable as a reverse commute. They also noted that apartments around 85 and Cottle are convenient but not exciting.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said San Jose is not very walkable. They said downtown has limited grocery options and the poster may need to drive to other neighborhoods for shopping.

[COMMENT]
A commenter suggested southern Santa Clara near Saratoga Avenue, Stevens Creek Boulevard, and San Tomas Expressway because utilities may be cheaper and the area has apartments, walkable services, and freeway access.
