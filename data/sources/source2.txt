Title: 2 bedroom 2 bath Apartment Recommendations South San Jose - Budget 4.5k all inclusive
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/SanJose/comments/1m0s17v/2_bedroom_2_bath_apartment_recommendations_south/
Type: Reddit thread

Original Post:
The poster and spouse are moving to San Jose in early August. One person works in Mountain View and the other travels to Monterey almost every day, so they are considering a 2 bed / 2 bath apartment around Santa Teresa. Their budget is about $4.5k all inclusive except electricity and internet. Options listed include DUO, Ascent, Lex, Vio, One Pearl Place, Bell Almaden, Monterey Grove, and Latitude 37. Requirements include two parking spots, a pool, a gym, and a safe neighborhood for evening walks or runs. They want to avoid insensitive or inefficient management.

[COMMENT]
A commenter who lived at VIO for about four years said Lex and Ascent are close by. The area has easy freeway access, but large apartment complexes can bring more tenant issues and theft. Safeway, restaurants, Bank of America, and Chase are within walking distance. The Monterey commute goes against traffic but can still become exhausting, especially if there is an accident near Salinas. Mountain View on 85 can be an hour or more, especially Friday evenings.

[COMMENT]
The same commenter said the only issues at VIO were with one leasing manager who made excuses. They also said parking in the area is bad, and they paid extra for a spot to make guest parking easier.

[COMMENT]
Another commenter also lived at VIO for four years and agreed with the earlier comment. They said guest parking is nonexistent and the leasing manager was useless but nice.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said their daughter lived at Ascent for the last year and liked it.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said the main Ascent issue involved maintenance access for fire sprinkler checks. The resident works night shift and wanted a smaller maintenance time window. Maintenance missed a scheduled time once or twice, but overall the apartments were described as clean and reasonably managed.

[COMMENT]
A commenter who lived at Ascent for almost three years said the complex became much worse over time. They complained about dog waste, rude people, overcrowding, and difficult parking.

[COMMENT]
The poster said their top two options were DUO and Ascent and asked whether a top floor would help avoid neighbor issues.

[COMMENT]
A commenter noted that the poster’s commute split is difficult because Mountain View and Monterey pull in different directions. The comment suggested taking Friday or Monday remote work if possible.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said the Santa Teresa / Cottle area is convenient for groceries and freeway access, but that it may not be exciting for nightlife or social activity.

[COMMENT]
Overall thread pattern: VIO, DUO, Lex, and Ascent are treated as convenient for South San Jose/Santa Teresa access, but commenters repeatedly mention parking, management, theft risk, and long regional commutes as concerns.
