Title: Apartments.com — 5 Best Neighborhoods in San Jose, CA for Renters
URL: https://www.apartments.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-san-jose-for-renters
Type: Neighborhood guide

Cleaned source notes:
The Apartments.com neighborhood guide frames San Jose as a major Bay Area city and Silicon Valley employment hub. It compares neighborhoods based on rental availability, average rent, proximity to employment or local points of interest, and neighborhood report-card style factors.

The guide lists Willow Glen, Downtown San Jose, South San Jose, Sun, and South Market among its renter-focused neighborhood options. For this project, the most relevant section is South San Jose.

South San Jose is described as quieter and more residential than downtown areas. It is presented as a place where renters may find more space, outdoor access, and apartment communities with amenities such as covered parking.

The guide connects South San Jose with access to Almaden Valley, Almaden Quicksilver County Park, trails, and regional shopping such as Eastridge Center. This makes South San Jose relevant for renters who care about outdoor access and everyday shopping more than nightlife.

For numerical context, the guide lists South San Jose as having an average one-bedroom rent of about $2,908 per month and an average one-bedroom size of about 714 square feet at the time captured.

The guide rates South San Jose as fairly walkable, having some public transit, highly drivable, and moderately bikeable. The key caution is that South San Jose is more car-dependent than neighborhoods closer to downtown, with less frequent transit coverage than the urban core.

Downtown San Jose is described as more walkable and transit-rich, with access to Diridon Station, SAP Center, museums, and San Pedro Square Market. However, renters should consider street noise and parking costs in downtown buildings.

Willow Glen is described as having a recognizable neighborhood identity around Lincoln Avenue, with coffee shops, boutiques, restaurants, and quieter residential streets. The main caution is limited availability and parking difficulty near popular restaurant blocks.
