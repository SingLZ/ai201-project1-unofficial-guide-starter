Title: Apartments that are Modern and Safe?
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/SanJose/comments/1fu57xx/apartments_that_are_modern_and_safe/
Type: Reddit thread

Original Post:
The poster is looking for a newer modern apartment with more space. They are concerned that many apartment reviews mention gun violence or other serious incidents near the complex. They are not expecting a crime-free area, but safety matters because they have a young family. They want recommendations for newer buildings with air conditioning, in-unit washer/dryer, and relative safety. In-unit laundry and A/C are preferred but not absolute deal breakers.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Crescent Village in North San Jose for families. They said they had lived there for four years and saw few incidents other than car break-ins, which they considered hard to avoid anywhere. They also liked unlimited underground visitor parking and a park across the street.

[COMMENT]
The poster said their budget was ideally less than or around $4,000 per month, but they could pay more for the right place. They wanted a two-bedroom if possible, but a one-bedroom could also work.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended Spring Creek in Santa Clara.

[COMMENT]
A commenter recommended 300 Railway in Campbell. They said it was built in 2019 and had a listed two-bedroom/two-bath unit around $4,100 with washer/dryer and air conditioning. They liked the location near Los Gatos Creek Trail, downtown Campbell, Campbell Park, the Pruneyard, and Safeway.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said Crescent Village units felt outdated and that they moved there mainly for cheaper rent. For more modern apartments, they recommended Graham Apartments because of modern amenities and a good park within walking distance.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said Brookside Oaks Apartments might be better for a family. They said they used to commute past the area, often saw kids walking around, and thought the area was nice.

[COMMENT]
A commenter said Irvine Company apartments in San Jose are generally good, but rent increases may cause people to move often.

[COMMENT]
The poster clarified that they did not mean San Jose was unsafe overall. They were concerned because many apartment reviews mentioned violent crimes on the property or nearby within the last year or two. They also said they take reviews with a grain of salt because people are more likely to post after a bad experience.

[COMMENT]
The poster said they were open to an older, working-class property, but their partner preferred newer buildings because of recent maintenance and mold issues where they currently lived.

[COMMENT]
A commenter suggested trying Santa Clara as an alternative area.

[COMMENT]
Overall thread pattern: modernity, family safety, A/C, washer/dryer, parking, parks, and review reliability are the key factors. Suggested areas include North San Jose, Campbell, Santa Clara, and some family-oriented complexes.
